Ginger Crinkles

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2/3 cup Shortening
  • 1 cup Sugar
  • 1 Egg
  • 4 Tbsp. Molasses
  • 2 cups Flour
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 2 tsp. Baking Soda
  • 1 tsp. Cinnamon
  • 1 tsp. Ginger
  • Sugar (for rolling)

Instructions & Notes

Cream shortening and sugar; beat in egg and molasses.

Combine flour, salt, baking soda, cinnamon, and ginger; blend into creamed mixture to form a soft dough.

Shape dough into 1-inch balls and roll generously in sugar.

Place on ungreased cookie sheets 2 inches apart. Bake at 350°F for 12-15 minutes until crinkled and set.
