BUFFET CHICKEN

CHICKEN

Ingredients

  • 4 CHICKEN BREASTS
  • 1/4 CUP BUTTER
  • 1/4 CUP DIJON MUSTARD
  • 1 TSP. SALT
  • 1/8 TSP. GARLIC POWDER OR CRUSHED GARLIC
  • 1/2 CUP HONEY
  • 2 TSP. CURRY POWDER
  • 2 TSP LEMON OR LIME JUICE
  • 2 TBSP. PREPARED MUSTARD
  • MUM

Instructions & Notes

PREHEAT OVEN 350 MELT BUTTER IN SAUCE PAN. ADD REMAINING INGREDIENTS. POUR OVER CHICKEN. BAKE 45 MINS. BASTE OCCASSIONLY.
