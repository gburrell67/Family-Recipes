Extra Special Chocolate Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3/4 CUP SHORTENING
  • 1 3/4 CUPS BROWN SUGAR
  • 1 WHOLE EGG AND 3 EGG YOKES
  • 3 SQUARES UNSWEETENED CHOCOLATE
  • 1 1/2 TSP. VANILLA
  • 1 1/2 CUPS MILK
  • 2 1/2 CUPS FLOUR
  • 1 1/2 TSP. SODA
  • 1/2 TSP. SALT

Instructions & Notes

BAKE IN 2 LAYERS. 350 FOR 22- 25 MINS.
