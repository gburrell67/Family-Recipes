Snicker Doodle Coffee Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Flour
  • 3/4 cup Sugar
  • Pinch of Salt
  • 1/2 tsp. Cinnamon
  • 1 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1/2 cup Milk
  • 1 Egg
  • 1/2 cup melted Butter
  • Topping: Cinnamon and Sugar

Instructions & Notes

Sift together flour, sugar, salt, cinnamon, and baking powder.

Whisk milk, egg, and melted butter; pour into dry ingredients and mix well.

Spread into a greased square baking pan. Sprinkle top generously with cinnamon and sugar.

Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes.
