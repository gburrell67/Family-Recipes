Rhubarb Custard Pie

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 CUPS RHUBARB
  • 1 CUP SUGAR
  • 1 TBSP. CORN STARCH.
  • 3 EGG YOKES
  • CREAMY. ADD BEATEN EGG YOKES. COOL

Instructions & Notes

MIX SUGAR AND CORN STARCH TOGETHER.
ADD TO CUT UP RHUBARB. COOK UNTIL CREAMY
POUR INTO A BAKED PIE SHELL
MERINGE: 3 EGG WHITES 3 TBSP. WATER BEAT EGG WHITES AND WATER UNTIL THEY FORM STIFF PEAKS. ADD ABOUT 3 TBSP. SUGAR. COVER PIE. BAKE 425 UNTIL GOLDEN BROWN.
