Shortbread Cookies

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Corn Starch
  • 1/2 cup Icing Sugar
  • 1 cup Flour
  • 3/4 cup Butter
  • etc.

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven to 300°F. Sift corn starch, icing sugar and flour together. Work in butter with your hands until a smooth dough forms.

Shape into 1 inch balls and place on an ungreased cookie sheet. Flatten gently with a fork. Bake 300 20 to 25 mins, until pale golden. Cool on the sheet.
