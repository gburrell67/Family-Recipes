Whipped Scotch Cookies

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 lb BUTTER
  • 1 CUP ICING SUGAR
  • 1/2 CUP CORN STARCH
  • 3 CUPS FLOUR
  • 1 tsp VANILLA

Instructions & Notes

Mix together and beat until fluffy. Drop from a spoon on cookie sheet. Bake 2o -25 mins at 325
