Spicy Ranch Chicken

Source: Shilpa Uskokovic

Ingredients

  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 1/2 cups plain whole-milk yogurt
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 Tbsp. dried dill
  • 2 Tbsp. garlic powder
  • 2 Tbsp. onion powder
  • 1 Tbsp. hot sauce (such as Frank's RedHot)
  • 2 tsp. freshly ground pepper
  • 2 tsp. sugar
  • 2 1/2 tsp. Diamond Crystal or 1 1/2 tsp. Morton kosher salt, divided
  • 2 lb. thin-sliced skinless, boneless chicken breasts
  • Vegetable oil (for grill)
  • Coarsely chopped chives and/or dill (for serving; optional)

Instructions

  1. Make Dressing: Whisk lemon zest, juice, yogurt, mayonnaise, dried dill, garlic powder, onion powder, hot sauce, pepper, sugar, and 1 tsp. salt in a large bowl. Reserve 1/2 cup for serving.
  2. Prep & Marinate: Slice chicken into thin cutlets if using regular breasts. Add to remaining dressing with 1 1/2 tsp. salt and toss to coat. Marinate 30 mins to 12 hours.
  3. Grill: Prepare grill for medium-high indirect heat. Grill chicken over direct heat until dark marks appear (~4 mins), flip, and move to indirect heat until cooked through (~3 mins).
  4. Serve: Slice crosswise into 3–4 pieces, spoon reserved dressing on platter, arrange chicken on top, and scatter fresh herbs.
