Fabulous Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Butter
  • 1 cup White Sugar
  • 1 cup Brown Sugar
  • 2 Eggs
  • 2 cups Flour
  • 2 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 4 oz. Chocolate
  • 1 cup Milk
  • 1 cup chopped Walnuts
  • 2 tsp. Vanilla
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 1egg
  • 1 tsp. Vanilla 1 tsp. Lemon Juice
  • 1 1/3 cup Icing Sugar. After Icing Sprinkle with chopped Nuts. Let Cake Stand 24 Hours Before Cutting!!

Instructions & Notes

Combine ingredients as with any ordinary butter cake recipe. Bake in square cake pan. 3oo 45 mins. Cool. Icing: 1 1/2 oz. chocolate and 1/4 cup of butter melted together. Pinch of salt
