Peanut Butter Cookies

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2/3 cup Peanut Butter
  • 1/2 cup Shortening
  • 2/3 cup lightly packed Brown Sugar
  • 1/2 cup White Sugar
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 1/4 cups Flour
  • 1/2 tsp. Baking Soda
  • 1/4 tsp. Salt

Instructions & Notes

Cream peanut butter, shortening, brown sugar, and white sugar until light. Beat in egg.

Combine flour, baking soda, and salt; blend into creamed mixture.

Roll dough into 1-inch balls and place on baking sheet. Press each ball with the tines of a fork in a criss-cross pattern.

Bake at 350°F for 12-15 minutes until lightly browned.
