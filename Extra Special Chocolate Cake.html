Extra Special Chocolate Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup Shortening
  • 1 3/4 cups Brown Sugar
  • 1 whole Egg and 3 Egg Yolks
  • 3 Squares Unsweetened Chocolate
  • 1 1/2 tsp. Vanilla
  • 1 1/2 cups Milk
  • 2 1/2 cups Flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp. Soda
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease and flour two layer cake pans. Melt the chocolate and let cool.

Cream shortening and brown sugar. Beat in the whole egg and egg yolks. Blend in melted chocolate and vanilla. Sift flour, soda and salt and add alternately with the milk, beating until smooth.

Bake in 2 layers. 350 for 22- 25 mins. Cool before icing.
