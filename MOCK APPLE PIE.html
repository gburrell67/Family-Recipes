Mock Apple Pie

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Apple Juice
  • 1 cup White Sugar
  • 2 tsp. Cream of Tartar
  • 1/4 tsp. Cinnamon
  • 22 Ritz Crackers
  • 1 Unbaked 9-inch Pie Shell
  • Topping: 1/3 cup Butter
  • Topping: 1/3 cup Sugar
  • Topping: 3/4 cup Flour

Instructions & Notes

Combine apple juice, sugar, cream of tartar, and cinnamon in a saucepan. Stir and bring to a rolling boil.

Drop in Ritz crackers one by one. Do not stir! Boil gently for 2 minutes.

Pour cracker and syrup mixture carefully into unbaked pie shell.

Combine topping ingredients (butter, sugar, flour) with a fork until crumbly; sprinkle evenly over pie.

Bake at 450°F for 10-15 minutes or until pastry is golden brown and cooked through.
