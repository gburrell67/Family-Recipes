Spiced Beef

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 7 or 8 lbs. Point Brisket
  • 5 Tbsp. Salt
  • 5 Tbsp. Sugar
  • Dash of Cinnamon
  • 3 cloves of Garlic (sliced)
  • 2 Tbsp. Saltpetre
  • 5 Tbsp. Mixed Whole Spices

Instructions & Notes

Place meat on heavy foil. Wipe surface dry and rub with tenderizer. Take a sharp knife and poke small holes throughout the brisket; insert slices of garlic into the holes.

Mix salt, sugar, dash of cinnamon, saltpetre, and whole mixed spices thoroughly. Rub spices and salt deeply into both sides of the brisket.

Wrap foil tightly around the meat and refrigerate for 6 days, turning daily. Bake at 300°F for about 3 hours until tender.
