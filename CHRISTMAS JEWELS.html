Christmas Jewels

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Butter
  • 1/2 cup Brown Sugar
  • 1/2 cup White Sugar
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 Tbsp. Water
  • 1 tsp. Vanilla
  • 1 cup Flour
  • 1/2 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1/2 tsp. Baking Soda
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 1 1/2 cups Rolled Oats
  • 1/2 cup Gumdrops (chopped)
  • Shredded Coconut (for rolling)

Instructions & Notes

Cream butter with brown and white sugar until fluffy. Add egg, water, and vanilla; beat well.

Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt; stir into butter mixture. Fold in chopped gumdrops and rolled oats.

Chill dough for 1 hour. Shape into small balls and roll in shredded coconut.

Place on baking sheet and bake at 350°F for 15-20 minutes until lightly browned.
