Banana Chocolate Chip Muffins

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 EGGS
  • 1 CUP MASHED BANANAS
  • 1/3 CUP MELTED BUTTER
  • 1 3/4 CUP FLOUR
  • 1/4 CUP NUTS
  • 1/2 CUP SUGAR
  • 4 TSP. BAKING POWDER
  • 1/2 TSP. SALT
  • 1/2 CUP CHOCOLATE CHIPS

Instructions & Notes

BAKE 400 10 -15 MINS.
