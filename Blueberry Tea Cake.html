Blueberry Tea Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Flour
  • 2 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 1/4 cup Shortening
  • 3/4 cup Sugar
  • 2 cups Blueberries
  • 1 Egg beaten
  • 1/2 cup Milk
  • Topping 1/2 cup Sugar
  • 1/4 cup Flour
  • 1/2 tsp. Cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup Butter

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a 9 x 9 inch pan.

Cream shortening and sugar. Beat in egg. Sift flour, baking powder and salt and add alternately with the milk. Gently fold in blueberries. Spread in pan.

Topping: Mix sugar, flour and cinnamon. Cut in butter until crumbly. Sprinkle over batter.

Bake 375 for about 40-45 mins.
