Rhubarb Walnut Muffins

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup plus 2 Tbsp. packed Brown Sugar
  • 1/4 cup melted Butter
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 tsp. Vanilla
  • 1/2 cup Buttermilk
  • 3/4 cup diced Rhubarb
  • 1/2 cup chopped Walnuts
  • 1 1/4 cups Flour
  • 1/2 tsp. Baking Soda
  • 1/2 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1/4 tsp. Salt
  • Topping: 1 Tbsp. melted Butter
  • Topping: 1/4 cup Sugar
  • Topping: 3/4 tsp. Cinnamon

Instructions & Notes

In a bowl, combine brown sugar, melted butter, egg, vanilla, and buttermilk. Stir in diced rhubarb and chopped walnuts.

In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt. Gently fold into the wet mixture until just moistened.

Spoon batter into greased muffin tins. Brush tops with 1 Tbsp melted butter and sprinkle with cinnamon-sugar topping.

Bake at 350°-375°F for about 20 minutes.
