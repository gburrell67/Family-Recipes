Morning Glory Muffins

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 1/4 cup white sugar
  • 2 tsp. Baking soda
  • 2 tsp. cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • Stir in
  • 2 cups grated carrot
  • 1/2 cup raisins
  • 1/2 cup nuts
  • 1/2 cup coconut
  • 1 apple
  • peeled
  • cored and grated.

Instructions & Notes

In bowl beat 3 Eggs 1/2 cup salad oil 2 tsp. vanilla Stir into flour mixture until batter is just combined. Spoon into well greased muffin cups Bake 350 -20 mins
