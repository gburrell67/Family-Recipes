Gumdrop Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/2 CUP BUTTER
  • 2 EGGS
  • 1 CUP WHITE SUGAR
  • 1CUP ALL PURPOSE FLOUR
  • 1 CUP CAKE FLOUR
  • 2 TSP. BAKING POWDER
  • 1 TSP. VANILLA
  • SALT

Instructions & Notes

BAKE 350 50-60 MINS.
