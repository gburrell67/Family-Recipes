CHINESE CABBAGE SALAD

SALAD

Ingredients

  • 1 BUNCH NAPPA/CHINESE CABBAGE
  • 1 BUNCH GREEN ONIONS
  • CHOP
  • 1 PKG. MR NOODLE CRUSHED
  • ( NO SEASONING )
  • 1/2 PKG. SLICED ALMONDS (TOASTED)
  • DRESSING:
  • 1/2 CUP OIL
  • 2 TBSP.EACH OF SUGAR
  • SOYA SAUCE VINEGAR
  • KEEP 3PARTS SEPARATE TIL READY TO EAT
  • PATTI
