SUNDAY SPICE CAKE

CAKE

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 CUP FLOUR
  • 1 1/4 CUP SUGAR
  • 1 TSP. SALT
  • 1 TSP. CINNAMON
  • 1/2 TSP. EACH OF CLOVES
  • NUTMEG
  • 1/2 TSP. ALLSPICE
  • 1/3 CUP MOLLASES
  • 2/3 CUP MILK
  • BEAT VIGOROUSLY
  • STIR IN
  • 2 1/2 TSP BAKING POWDER
  • 1/2 TSP BAKING SODA
  • 3 EGGS
  • 1/2 CUP MILK
  • MUM

Instructions & Notes

COOK AS TWO LAYERS
OR IN A 9 X 13 PAN BAKE 375 FOR 35 MINS.
