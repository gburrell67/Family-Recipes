Pumpkin or Squash Pie

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Pumpkin or Squash
  • 1/2 cup Brown Sugar
  • 2 Tbsp. Molasses
  • 1 Tbsp. Butter (melted)
  • 1 Tbsp. Cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp. Ginger
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 cup Blend (top milk)
  • 1 tsp. Lemon
  • Add Sugar
  • Molasses
  • Spices

Instructions & Notes

Salt and melted butter to pumpkin or squash. Beat eggs slightly and add.then add milk and mix thoroughly. Put in an unbaked pie shell. Bake 475 for 10 mins then 325 for 45 mins
