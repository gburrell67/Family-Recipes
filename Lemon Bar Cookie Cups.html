Lemon Bar Cookie Cups

From Family Recipes • Tender Cookie Cup with Tangy Lemon Curd

Ingredients

  • --- Cookie Cups ---
  • 2 3/4 cups Flour
  • 1 tsp. Baking Soda
  • 1/2 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1 cup Butter (softened)
  • 1 1/4 cups Sugar
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 tsp. Vanilla
  • --- Lemon Curd ---
  • 6 Tbsp. Butter (softened)
  • 1 cup Sugar
  • 4 Eggs, divided (2 whole eggs + 2 egg yolks)
  • 2/3 cup Lemon Juice
  • --- Topping ---
  • Fresh Lemon Zest
  • Powdered Sugar (for dusting)

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven to 375°F. Line or lightly spray a muffin tin.

In a small bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients (flour, baking soda, and baking powder).

In a large mixing bowl, cream together softened butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Beat in the egg and vanilla extract. Gradually add dry ingredients to wet mixture, mixing until a dough forms.

Form dough into 1-inch balls and place one ball into each muffin cup. Bake for 8-10 minutes until lightly golden. Immediately upon removing from the oven, gently press down the center of each cookie with the back of a spoon to form a cup shape. Let cool completely in the pan.

Prepare Lemon Curd: In a saucepan, cream softened butter and sugar. Whisk in 2 whole eggs, 2 egg yolks, and lemon juice. Cook over medium heat, whisking constantly until the mixture thickens (about 5 minutes). Remove from heat and let cool slightly.

Spoon warm lemon curd into the cooled cookie cups. Top with freshly grated lemon zest and chill in the refrigerator until set.

Gently remove cookie cups from tin and dust generously with powdered sugar before serving.
