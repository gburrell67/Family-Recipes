Cheese Ball

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 packages (250 g each) Cream Cheese
  • 3 or 4 Green Onions (diced fine)
  • 2 or 3 Dashes of Worcestershire Sauce
  • 2 or 3 Dashes of Soya Sauce
  • 2 or 3 Dashes of Tabasco Sauce
  • 1 pkg. of Summer Sausage or Salami

Instructions & Notes

Mix cream cheese, onion and sauces all together. Take 1/2 of the salami and dice very small. Add to cheese mixture. Roll cheese mixture into 2 balls. Then roll in remaining salami, which has been finely chopped.
