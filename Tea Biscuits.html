Tea Biscuits

Source: Printed Cookbook

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all purpose flour
  • 4 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup shortening
  • 1 cup milk

Instructions

Preheat oven to 425F.
Sift together flour, baking powder, and salt.
Cut in shortening finely with a pastry blender or two knives.
Add milk and stir with a fork to make a soft dough.
Turn dough onto a lightly floured surface and knead gently 8 to 10 times.
Roll or pat to desired thickness.
Cut with a floured 1 3/4-inch cookie cutter.
Place biscuits on an ungreased baking sheet, close together for soft-sided biscuits or about 1 inch apart for crusty-sided biscuits.
Bake in preheated 425F oven for 12 to 15 minutes.
Makes 20 biscuits.
