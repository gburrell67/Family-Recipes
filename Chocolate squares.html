Chocolate Squares

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 CUP SOFT BUTTER
  • 1 1/2 CUPS SUGAR
  • 3 EGGS
  • 1 TSP.VANILLA
  • 1 TSP BAKING POWDER
  • 1 CUP FLOUR
  • 1/4 CUP COCOA
  • 1/2 CUP WALNUTS
  • COVER WITH 1 CAN SWEETENED CONDENCED

Instructions & Notes

MIX ABOVE INGREDIENTS AND SPREAD IN
A 13 X10 PAN. BAKE 350 FOR ABOUT 30 MINS
MILK AND 1 CUP COCONUT MIX TOGETHER
AND SPREAD OVER THE TOP. RETURN TO OVEN AND BAKE UNTIL LIGHTLY BROWNED 15-20 MINS. ICING 2 CUPS ICING SUGAR 1/4 CUP COCOA 2 TBSP.BUTTER 1/2 TSP.VANILLA 3 TBSP. CREAM
