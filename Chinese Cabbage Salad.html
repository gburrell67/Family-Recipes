Chinese Cabbage Salad

From Patti's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch Nappa / Chinese Cabbage (chopped)
  • 1 bunch Green Onions (chopped)
  • 1 pkg. Mr. Noodle (crushed, no seasoning)
  • 1/2 pkg. sliced Almonds (toasted)
  • Dressing: 1/2 cup Oil
  • Dressing: 2 Tbsp. Sugar
  • Dressing: 2 Tbsp. Soya Sauce
  • Dressing: 2 Tbsp. Vinegar

Instructions & Notes

Chop cabbage and green onions. Toast sliced almonds in a dry pan until fragrant.

Whisk dressing ingredients (oil, sugar, soya sauce, and vinegar) until sugar dissolves.

Keep cabbage mixture, crunchy noodles/almonds, and dressing in separate containers until ready to serve.

Toss all 3 parts together right before eating so noodles stay crisp.
