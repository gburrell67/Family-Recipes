Carrot Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 CUPS SUGAR
  • 4 EGGS
  • 2 CUPS FLOUR
  • 2 TSP. BAKING POWDER
  • 1 TSP. SODA
  • 1 TSP. SALT
  • 2 TSP. CINNAMON
  • 3 CUPS GRATED RAW CARROTS
  • 1 CUP FINELY CHOPPED WALNUTS

Instructions & Notes

1 CUP COOKING OIL
BAKE 325 FOR ABOUT 1 HOUR
CREAM CHEESE ICING 1 8OZ. PACKAGE CREAM CHEESE 1 TBSP. COOKING OIL 1 TSP. VANILLA 3 1/2 CUPS ICING SUGAR 1/4 TSP. SALT
