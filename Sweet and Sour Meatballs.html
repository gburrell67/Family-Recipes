Sweet and Sour Meatballs

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs. Ground Meat
  • 1 Onion (finely chopped)
  • Salt and Pepper to taste
  • 1 cup Ketchup
  • 1 Tbsp. Dry Mustard
  • 3/4 cup Vinegar
  • 3/4 cup Water
  • 2 Tbsp. Flour
  • 1 1/2 cups Brown Sugar

Instructions & Notes

Shape ground meat and onion seasoned with salt and pepper into meatballs and place in a baking dish.

In a bowl, whisk together ketchup, dry mustard, vinegar, water, flour, and brown sugar until smooth to make the sweet and sour sauce.

Pour sauce generously over meatballs. Bake uncovered at 350°F for 1 hour.
