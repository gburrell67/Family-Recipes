Barbecued Pork Chops

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 medium Onion chopped
  • 1/2 cup Water
  • 1/4 cup Vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp. Lemon Juice
  • 2 Tbsp. Brown Sugar
  • 2 tsp. Dry Mustard
  • 2 tsp. Salt
  • 1/2 tsp. Pepper
  • 1/2 cup Ketchup
  • 2 tsp. Worcestershire Sauce
  • 4 Pork Chops

Instructions & Notes

Saute onion with salt and pepper in a saucepan until onion is tender. Add water, vinegar, lemon juice, brown sugar, dry mustard, ketchup and Worcestershire sauce. Bring to a boil. Remove from heat. Place pork chops in a baking dish. Pour half the sauce over the pork chops. Bake uncovered at 350 for 30 mins. turn and bake 20-30 mins. longer.
