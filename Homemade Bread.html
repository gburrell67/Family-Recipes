Homemade Bread

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2/3 cup Milk
  • 4 tsp. Sugar
  • 2 tsp. Shortening
  • 2/3 cup Water
  • 2 tsp. Salt
  • 1 Tbsp. Yeast
  • 8 tsp. Warm Water (for yeast)
  • 3 cups White Flour
  • 1 cup Whole Wheat Flour

Instructions & Notes

Soak yeast in warm water (8 teaspoons). Add to bread machine (2 lb loaf) or mix by hand.

Warm milk and melt shortening. Mix and knead thoroughly until dough is smooth and elastic. Let rise until doubled in bulk. Punch down, let rise until doubled again, divide into 3 loaves, and let rise once more.

Bake in 400°F oven for 30-40 minutes. (To make full recipe, multiply ingredients by 3.)
