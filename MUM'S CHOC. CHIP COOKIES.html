Mum's Chocolate Chip Cookies

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Butter
  • 1 cup Shortening
  • 2 cups Brown Sugar
  • 2 Eggs
  • 4 cups Flour
  • 2 tsp. Baking Soda
  • 4 tsp. Cream of Tartar
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 1 tsp. Vanilla
  • 1 to 2 cups Chocolate Chips

Instructions & Notes

Cream butter, shortening, and brown sugar until smooth. Beat in eggs and vanilla.

Sift flour, baking soda, cream of tartar, and salt together; blend into creamed mixture. Fold in chocolate chips.

Drop rounded spoonfuls onto an ungreased cookie sheet.

Bake at 375°F for about 12 minutes until edges are golden.
