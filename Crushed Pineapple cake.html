Crushed Pineapple Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3/4 CUP BUTTER
  • 1 CUP SUGAR
  • 2 LARGE EGGS
  • 2 3/4 CUPS FLOUR
  • 2 tsp. BAKING POWDER
  • 1 tsp. SALT
  • 2 cups White RAISINS
  • 1 CUP RED CHERRIES
  • 1 CUP GREEN CHERRIES
  • 15 oz. CAN PINEAPPLE

Instructions & Notes

1 CUP MIXED FRUIT
Drain Pineapple and mix fruits together. Follow the usual cake method adding fruit last. Line pan with wax paper greased. Let stand overnight and bake 275 for about 3 hours.
