Spicey Raisin Oatmeal Cookies

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/2 CUP SHORTENING
  • 1/2 CUP BROWN SUGAR
  • 1/2 CUP WHITE SUGAR
  • 1 EGG
  • 1/2 TSP VANILLA
  • 2 TBSP. MILK
  • 1 CUP FLOUR 1 TSP. SODA
  • 1/2 TSP. SALT 1/2 TSP. CINNAMON
  • 1/2 TSP. NUTMEG
  • 1 1/2 CUPS OATMEAL
  • 1 CUP RAISINS

Instructions & Notes

BAKE 350 FOR 12-15 MINS
