Pavlova

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 4 Egg Whites
  • 1 cup Granulated Sugar
  • 1 tsp. Corn Starch
  • 1 tsp. Vinegar
  • 1 tsp Vanilla
  • Beat Egg Whites Until Soft Peaks Form. Add Sugar and Corn Starch Gradually

Instructions & Notes

Beating until stiff. Beat in vinegar and vanilla. There should not be any grainy feel when the whites are rubbed between fingers. Place foil on a cookie sheet. Spread meringue in a circle. Bake 250 for 50 to 60 mins. turn off heat and leave in oven for 1 hour. Cool. Top with sweetened whipped cream and fruit. I use a glaze of strawberry jam over the fruit.
