Apple Crunch

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 5 or 6 medium Apples
  • 1/3 cup Flour
  • 3/4 cup Brown Sugar
  • 1/3 cup Butter
  • 1 cup Special K Cereal

Instructions & Notes

Peel and cut apples into small pieces. Place evenly in a greased baking dish.

Combine butter, flour, and brown sugar until crumbly, then gently fold in Special K cereal.

Spread cereal mixture evenly over apples.

Bake at 375°F for about 30 minutes until apples are tender and top is golden and crunchy.
