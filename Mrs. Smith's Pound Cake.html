Mrs. Smith's Pound Cake

From Mrs. Smith's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Butter
  • 1 cup Sugar
  • Cream Together
  • 2 cups Flour
  • 1 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1/2 cup Milk
  • 1 tsp. Vanilla
  • 1 tsp. Lemon
  • 3 Eggs
  • Cream Butter and Sugar
  • Add Flour
  • Baking Powder
  • Milk
  • Vanilla

Instructions & Notes

And lemon. Beat 2 mins. add 3 eggs beat 2 mins. bake 350 for about 60 mins
