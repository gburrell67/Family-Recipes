Cream Cheese Biscuits

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Flour
  • 1 Tbsp. Baking Powder
  • 1 Tub (250 g.) Spreadable Cream Cheese
  • 1/2 cup Milk
  • etc.
  • You May Add 1/2 cup of grated
  • Cheddercheese
  • Orange or Lemon
  • Zest
  • Dried Cranberries
  • Raisins
  • OR
  • Chocolate Chips.

Instructions & Notes

Mix flour and baking powder. Cut in cream cheese to form a crumbly mixture. Stir in milk and desired add- ins. Pat dough to 3/4 thick on a floured board.cut biscuits. Bake 425 12-15 mins.
