Rhubarb Sour Cream Pie

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • Filling:
  • 4 cups chopped Rhubarb
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 1/2 cups Granulated Sugar
  • 1 cup Sour Cream
  • 1/3 cup Flour
  • Topping:
  • 1/2 cup Flour
  • 1/2 cup Brown Sugar
  • 1/4 cup Butter (melted)
  • Arrange Rhubarb Evenly in Pie Shell. in Bowl
  • Wisk Together Egg
  • Sugar
  • Sour Cream

Instructions & Notes

Unbaked pie shell
and flour until smooth. Pour over fruit. Topping: in small bowl mix together flour and sugar. Drizzle in butter and mix until crumbly. Sprinkle over filling. Bake 450 for 15 mins then 350 for about 40 mins.
