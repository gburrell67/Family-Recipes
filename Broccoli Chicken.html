Broccoli Chicken

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 Chicken (cooked and cut up)
  • Broccoli (cooked until tender)
  • 1 cup Mayonnaise
  • 1 can Cream of Chicken Soup
  • 1 can Cheddar Cheese Soup
  • 4 Tbsp. Lemon Juice
  • Pepper
  • Paprika
  • Parmesan Cheese

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 13" x 9" pan.

Arrange cooked broccoli in the pan and top with the cooked, cut up chicken. Mix mayonnaise, cream of chicken soup, cheddar cheese soup and lemon juice and pour over the chicken. Sprinkle with pepper, Parmesan cheese and paprika.

Bake 350 for 30 mins in a 13" x 9" pan, until bubbly.
