Mrs. Smith's Pound Cake

From Mrs. Smith's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 CUP BUTTER
  • 1 CUP SUGAR
  • CREAM TOGETHER
  • 2 CUPS FLOUR
  • 1 TSP. BAKING POWDER
  • 1/2 CUP MILK
  • 1 TSP. VANILLA
  • 1 TSP. LEMON
  • 3 EGGS
  • CREAM BUTTER AND SUGAR
  • ADD FLOUR
  • BAKING POWDER
  • MILK
  • VANILLA

Instructions & Notes

AND LEMON. BEAT 2 MINS. ADD 3 EGGS BEAT 2 MINS. BAKE 350 FOR ABOUT 60 MINS
