Never Fail Pastry

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 6 cups Flour
  • 1 lb. Lard
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 Egg
  • 1tbsp. Vinegar
  • 1 cup Water
  • Lard
  • And Salt Until small Crumbs. in Measuring cup Beat Egg
  • Add Vinegar and Water to Make 1cup.

Instructions & Notes

Mix flour, lard, and salt until small crumbs. In measuring cup beat egg, add vinegar and water to make 1 cup.

Add liquid to the flour mixture and stir with a fork until the dough just holds together. Divide into portions, wrap and chill about 30 minutes. (Keeps in the refrigerator for 2-3 weeks or may be frozen.)

Roll out on a floured surface and bake according to your pie recipe.

Note: Remaining steps were added from classic similar recipes.
