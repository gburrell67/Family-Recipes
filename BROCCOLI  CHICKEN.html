Broccoli Chicken

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 CUP MAYONAISE
  • 1 CAN CREAM OF CHICKEN SOUP
  • 1 CAN CHEDDAR CHEESE SOUP
  • 4 TBSP. LEMON JUICE
  • PEPPER
  • PAPRIKA
  • PARMESAN CHEESE

Instructions & Notes

1 CHICKEN (COOKED AND CUT UP)
BROCCOLI ( COOKED UNTIL TENDER)
BAKE 350 30 MINS. 13"" X9"" PAN
