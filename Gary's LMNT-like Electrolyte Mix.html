Gary's LMNT-like Electrolyte Mix

Source: Gary

Ingredients

  • 45.0 grams Sodium Chloride (Plain Salt)
  • 45.0 grams Nu-Salt (Potassium Chloride)
  • 13.5 grams Magnesium Malate (Fine Powder)
  • 7.2 grams Monk Fruit Extract Powder (Pure 100%)
  • 144.0 grams True Lemon Bulk Powder
  • 144.0 grams True Lime Bulk Powder
  • 45.0 grams Citric Acid

Instructions

Combine all ingredients in a large, clean, dry container and whisk or shake vigorously until the powders are completely uniform.
Store tightly sealed in an airtight jar with a silica gel packet to prevent moisture clumping.
Total batch weight: 443.7 grams.
Single serving dosage: 4.93 grams per serving.
