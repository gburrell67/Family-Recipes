Shortbread Meltaways

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Butter
  • 1/2 cup Icing Sugar
  • 2 tsp. Vanilla
  • 1 3/4 cup Flour
  • 1/2 cup Corn Starch
  • 3/4 tsp.Baking Powder
  • 1/4 tsp.Salt
  • ! cup Skor Toffee Bits

Instructions & Notes

Roll in 1"" balls. Bake350 about 15 mins When cool may be dusted with Icing Sugar.
