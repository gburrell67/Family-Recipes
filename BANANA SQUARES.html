Banana Squares

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/4 CUP BUTTER
  • 1/4 CUP SOUR MILK
  • 1/2 TSP. BAKING SODA
  • 1 CUP FLOUR
  • 3/4 CUP WHITE SUGAR
  • 1 EGG
  • 1/2 TSP. BAKING POWDER
  • 1/2 CUP MASHED BANANAS

Instructions & Notes

BAKE 350 - 30 MINS.
