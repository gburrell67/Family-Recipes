Aunt Edna's Mincemeat

From Aunt Edna's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • --- Quick Recipe ---
  • 1 lb. Seedless Raisins
  • 1/2 can Apple Juice
  • 1/2 cup Vinegar
  • Brown Sugar to taste
  • Cinnamon, Cloves, and Allspice to taste
  • --- Traditional Heirloom Recipe ---
  • 5 cups Cooked Meat (2 lbs., ground)
  • 2 1/2 cups Suet (1 lb., uncooked)
  • 7 1/2 cups Apples (ground)
  • 3 cups Apple Juice
  • 1/2 cup Vinegar
  • 3/4 lb. Citron
  • 1 cup Molasses
  • 5 cups Sugar
  • 1 1/2 cups Raisins
  • 1 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • Juice of 2 Lemons and 2 Oranges
  • 1 Tbsp. Mace
  • 2 Tbsp. each Cinnamon, Cloves, Allspice
  • 2 Nutmeg (grated)

Instructions & Notes

Grind cooked meat. Put apples through meat grinder. Combine with uncooked suet, apple juice, vinegar, citron, molasses, sugar, raisins, salt, citrus juices, and ground spices.

Cook mixture slowly until apples and suet are completely transparent, stirring frequently while cooking. Add a little water if needed.

Seal in 16 oz jars for holiday mincemeat pies.
