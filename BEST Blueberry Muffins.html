Best Blueberry Muffins

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups Sugar
  • 1/2 cup Butter
  • 2 Eggs
  • 2 cups Flour
  • 2 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 1/2 cup Milk
  • 2 cups Blueberries
  • Sugar for sprinkling on top

Instructions & Notes

Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add beaten eggs and blend well.

Whisk flour, baking powder, and salt together; add to creamed mixture alternately with milk until just blended.

Carefully fold in fresh blueberries.

Spoon into greased muffin cups and sprinkle tops lightly with sugar.

Bake at 350°F for 20-25 minutes until tops spring back when lightly touched.
