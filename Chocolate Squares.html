Chocolate Squares

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • --- Base ---
  • 1 cup Soft Butter
  • 1 1/2 cups Sugar
  • 3 Eggs
  • 1 tsp. Vanilla
  • 1 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1 cup Flour
  • 1/4 cup Cocoa
  • 1/2 cup Walnuts
  • --- Topping ---
  • 1 can Sweetened Condensed Milk
  • 1 cup Coconut
  • --- Icing ---
  • 2 cups Icing Sugar
  • 1/4 cup Cocoa
  • 2 Tbsp. Butter
  • 1/2 tsp. Vanilla
  • 3 Tbsp. Cream

Instructions & Notes

Mix base ingredients and spread in a 13 x 10 pan. Bake 350 for about 30 mins.

Mix sweetened condensed milk and coconut together and spread over the top. Return to oven and bake until lightly browned, 15-20 mins.

Mix icing ingredients and spread over the squares.
