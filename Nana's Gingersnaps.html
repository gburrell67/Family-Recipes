Nana's Gingersnaps

From Nana's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Shortening
  • 1 cup Molasses
  • 1 cup Brown Sugar
  • 1/2 cup White Sugar
  • 2 tsp. Ginger
  • 1 tsp. Salt
  • 1 Tbsp. Soda
  • 1 Egg
  • 2 tsp. Vinegar
  • 3 1/2 cup of Flour
  • Heat Molasses to Boiling Point. (i put it in micro wave for 1 min) Add Shortening

Instructions & Notes

Heat molasses to boiling point (1 min in microwave). Add shortening, then add remaining ingredients. Chill dough until firm.

Roll into 1 inch balls and place on an ungreased cookie sheet. Bake 350-375°F for about 10-12 mins.

Note: Remaining steps were added from classic similar recipes.
