PEACH UPSIDE DOWN CAKE

Appetizer

Ingredients

  • 1/3 CUP BUTTER
  • 1/2 CUP BROWN SUGAR
  • 3 TBSP. CORN SYRUP
  • 1/4 CUP SHORTENING
  • 1/2 CUP SUGAR
  • 2 EGGS
  • 1/4 CUP ALMOND EXTRACT
  • 1 1/2 TSP. BAKING POWDER
  • 1/4 TSP. SALT
  • 1/4 CUP MILK
  • PUT BUTTER AND SUGAR IN A NINE INCH PAN
  • LETTING ALL JUICES DRIP OVER CAKE.
  • MUM

Instructions & Notes

PUT OVER LOW HEAT TO BLEND AND COOK JUST UNTIL MIXTURE BUBBLES. ADD CORN SRRUP. PLACE SLICED PEACHES IN THE MIXTURE. MAKE CAKE MIXTURE AND SPOON BATTER OVER PEACHES BAKE 350 35 MINS. COOL ON CAKE RACK 10 MINS. THEN INVERT OVER SERVING PLATE
