Orange Squares

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup Sugar
  • 2 cups Flour
  • 1 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 2 cups Fine Coconut
  • 1 cup Butter
  • Filling:
  • 1 cup White Sugar
  • 2 Tbsp. Corn Starch
  • Juice and Rind of One Orange
  • 1 cup of Hot Water
  • 1 Tbsp. Butter
  • Water
  • Sugar
  • Flour
  • Baking Powder
  • Salt
  • Coconut

Instructions & Notes

Cook orange juice
and corn starch until thick. Add butter. Mix sugar
and butter together.place half the mixture in 9 x 11 pan. Cover with filling. Then the rest of the mixture bake 30 mins 375
