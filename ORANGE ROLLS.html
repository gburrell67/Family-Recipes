Orange Rolls

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 ENVELOPE OF YEAST (1 TBSP)
  • 1/4 CUP WARM WATER
  • 1/4 CUP SUGAR
  • 1 TSP. SALT
  • 2 EGGS
  • 1/2 CUP SOUR CREAM
  • 6 TBSP. BUTTER
  • 3 1/2 CUPS FLOUR
  • GLAZE: 3/4 CUP SUGAR
  • 1/2 CUP SOUR CREAM
  • 2 TBSP. ORANGE RIND
  • 1/2 CUP BUTTER
  • SOAK YEAST IN !/4 CUP WARM WATER. ADD SUGAR
  • SALT
  • EGGS
  • SOUR CREAM

Instructions & Notes

BUTTER AND ONE CUP OF FLOUR. ADD REMAINING FLOUR UNTIL DOUGH IS SMOOTH AND NOT STICKY. ALLOW TO RAISE IN A WARM PLACE UNTIL DOUBLE IN BULK. DIVIDE DOUGH IN HALF. ROLL INTO ABOUT 10"" CIRCLE. CUT IN 12 PIE SHAPE PIECES. PLACE IN 9X 13"" GREASED PAN. ALLOW TO RAISE AGAIN. BAKE 350 20 MINS. COVER WITH GLAZE WHICH HAS BEEN COOKED UNTIL THICK
