Prize Ginger Bread

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/2 CUP SUGAR
  • 1 EGG
  • 1 CUP MOLASSES
  • 2 1/2 CUPS FLOUR
  • 1 1/2 TSP. SODA
  • 1 TSP. GINGER
  • 1 TSP. CINNAMON
  • 1/2 TSP. SALT
  • 1/2 TSP. CLOVES
  • 1 CUP HOT WATER
  • CREAM SHORTENING

Instructions & Notes

1/2 CUP SHORTENING AND BUTTER MIXED
BUTTER AND SUGAR. ADD EGG AND MOLASSES. THEN DRY INGREDIENTS. ADD HOT WATER LAST AND BEAT UNTIL SMOOTH. BAKE IN A GREASED 9X9 PAN 350 FOR ABOUT 35 MINS.
