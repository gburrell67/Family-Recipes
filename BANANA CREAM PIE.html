Banana Cream Pie

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3/4 CUP SUGAR
  • 3 TBSP. CORN STARCH
  • 1/2 TSP. SALT
  • 2 CUPS MILK
  • 3 EGG YOKES
  • 1 TSP. VANILLA
  • MERINGNE:
  • 3 EGG WHITES
  • 3 TBSP. WATER
  • 1/4 CUP SUGAR
  • DASH OF SALT
  • CORN STARCH
  • SALT TOGETHER.

Instructions & Notes

MIX SUGAR
IN A SAUCE PAN. COOK OVER MEDIUM HEAT UNTIL IT BEGINS TO THICKEN. ADD EGG YOKES
BEATING WELL. COOK UNTIL THICK. ADD VANILLA. COOL PLACE 1/3 OF FILLING IN BAKED PIE SHELL. ADD LAYER OF BANANAS . REPEAT. FINISHING WITH FILLING. COVER WITH MERINGNE. BAKE 425 UNTIL MERINGNE IS GOLDEN BROWN.
