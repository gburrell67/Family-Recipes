Chocolate Snowballs

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3/4 CUP BUTTER
  • 1/2 CUP SUGAR
  • 2 TSP. VANILLA
  • 1 EGG
  • 2 CUPS FLOUR
  • 1/2 TSP. SALT
  • 1 6 OZ. PKG. CHOCOLATE CHIPS
  • 1 CUP CHOPPED NUTS
  • BLEND BUTTER
  • SUGAR

Instructions & Notes

VANILLA. BEAT IN EGG. STIR IN FLOUR MIXTURE
CHOCOLATE CHIPS AND NUTS. SHAPE INTO 1 INCH BALLS. PLACE ON UNGREASED COOKIE SHEET BAKE 350 15-20 MINS.
