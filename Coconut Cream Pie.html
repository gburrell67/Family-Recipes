Coconut Cream Pie

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • --- Filling ---
  • 3/4 cup Sugar
  • 3 Tbsp. Corn Starch
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 2 cups Milk
  • 3 Egg Yolks
  • 1 cup Shredded Coconut
  • --- Meringue ---
  • 3 Egg Whites
  • 3 Tbsp. Water
  • 1/4 cup Sugar

Instructions & Notes

Mix sugar, corn starch and salt in a saucepan. Add milk. Cook until it begins to thicken. Add egg yolks and beat well. Cook until thick. Remove from heat. Add coconut. Cool. Place in a baked pie shell.

Cover with meringue. Bake 425 until meringue is golden brown.
