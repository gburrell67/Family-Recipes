Prize Ginger Bread

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Sugar
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 cup Molasses
  • 2 1/2 cups Flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp. Soda
  • 1 tsp. Ginger
  • 1 tsp. Cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 1/2 tsp. Cloves
  • 1 cup Hot Water
  • Cream Shortening

Instructions & Notes

1/2 cup shortening and butter mixed
Butter and sugar. Add egg and molasses. Then dry ingredients. Add hot water last and beat until smooth. Bake in a greased 9x9 pan 350 for about 35 mins.
