Gumdrop Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Butter
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 cup White Sugar
  • 1cup All Purpose Flour
  • 1 cup Cake Flour
  • 2 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1 tsp. Vanilla
  • Salt

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a loaf or tube pan.

Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Beat in eggs one at a time, then vanilla. Stir together all purpose flour, cake flour, baking powder and salt and blend into the creamed mixture. Fold in chopped gumdrops that have been tossed in a little flour so they do not sink.

Spoon batter into pan. Bake 350 50-60 mins, until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool in pan 10 minutes before turning out.
