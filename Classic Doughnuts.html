Doughnuts

Classic Doughnuts

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3 Eggs (well beaten)
  • 1 cup White Sugar
  • 3 Tbsp. Butter (melted)
  • 1 tsp. Salt
  • 1 cup Milk
  • 3 1/2 cups Flour
  • 4 tsp. Baking Powder (well rounded)
  • 1 tsp. Vanilla
  • 1/2 tsp. Nutmeg

Instructions & Notes

Beat eggs well, add sugar, and stir in milk. Add sifted dry ingredients and melted butter. (This dough is soft, making wonderfully light doughnuts.)

Chill dough until easy to handle. Roll out on a floured surface to 1/2-inch thickness and cut with doughnut cutter.

Fry in deep hot fat (370°F) until golden brown, turning once. Drain and dust with sugar or cinnamon-sugar if desired.
