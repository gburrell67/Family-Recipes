Dark Fruit Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup Seedless Raisins
  • 1 cup Currants
  • 1 cup Separated Seeded Raisins
  • 2 cups Candied Cherries
  • 1 cup Almonds
  • 1 cup Dates
  • Batter: 2 2/3 cup Flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 3/4 tsp. Salt 1 1/2 tsp Cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp. Ginger 1/4 tsp. Cloves

Instructions & Notes

1/2 cup mixed peel
1 1/2 cups mixed fruit
1 cup butter 1 1/4 cup b. sugar 6 eggs 1/4 cup molasses 1/3 cup strong coffee makes two loaf pans. Grease pans and line with brown paper. Baste top of cake with milk
While baking. Place pan of water in oven while baking. Bake 300 for about 3 hours.
