Canadian Style Tea Biscuits

Source: Handwritten Note

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • Salt
  • 4 tbsp shortening
  • 1 cup milk

Instructions

Blend flour, baking powder, salt, and shortening well.
Add milk and stir to combine.
Bake at 400F for 8-10 mins.
