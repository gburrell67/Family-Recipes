Date Pinwheels

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 CUPS BROWN SUGAR
  • 3/4 CUP BUTTER
  • 2 EGGS
  • 3 CUPS FLOUR
  • 1/2 TSP. CREAM OF TARTER
  • 1 TSP. SODA
  • SALT
  • VANILLA
  • FILLING : 3/4 LB. DATES
  • 1/4 CUP SUGAR
  • 1 1/4 CUP WATER

Instructions & Notes

COOK FILLING. COOL. DIVIDE DOUGH IN 4 PIECES. ROLL TO ABOUT 1/4 INCH. SPREAD WITH FILLING. ROLL LIKE A JELLY ROLL. FREEZE
THEN SLICE AND BAKE. 350 10-15 MINS.
