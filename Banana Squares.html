Banana Squares

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup Butter
  • 1/4 cup Sour Milk
  • 1/2 tsp. Baking Soda
  • 1 cup Flour
  • 3/4 cup White Sugar
  • 1 Egg
  • 1/2 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1/2 cup Mashed Bananas

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9 x 9 inch pan.

Cream butter and sugar until light. Beat in egg. Dissolve baking soda in the sour milk. Stir flour and baking powder together and add to the creamed mixture alternately with the sour milk. Stir in mashed bananas.

Spread batter in pan. Bake 350 - 30 mins. Cool and cut into squares.
