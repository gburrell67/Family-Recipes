Bohemian Braid

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Warm Water
  • 2 pkgs. Dry Yeast
  • 3/4 cup Lukewarm Milk
  • 1/4 cup Sugar
  • 1 tsp. Salt
  • 1/3 cup Shortening
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 tsp. grated Lemon Rind
  • 1/4 tsp. Mace
  • 1/2 cup Raisins
  • 1/2 cup chopped Candied Fruit
  • 1/2 cup slivered Almonds
  • 4 1/4 to 4 3/4 cups Flour

Instructions & Notes

Soak yeast in 1/2 cup warm water for 5 minutes. Beat in milk, sugar, salt, shortening, eggs, lemon rind, and mace with 1 cup flour until smooth.

Stir in candied fruit, raisins, and almonds. Gradually add remaining flour until dough forms. Knead on floured surface until smooth and elastic. Place in greased bowl, cover, and let rise until doubled.

Divide dough into 4 parts. Shape 3 parts into 9-inch ropes and braid them into a base loaf. Divide the 4th part into 3 smaller ropes, braid, and place on top of base loaf. Let rise 45-60 minutes.

Bake at 350°F for 40-50 minutes. While warm, frost with thin vanilla icing and garnish with candied cherries.
