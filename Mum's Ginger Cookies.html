Mum's Ginger Cookies

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Brown Sugar
  • 1/2 cup White
  • 1 cup Molasses
  • 1 cup Shortening
  • 1 tsp. Salt
  • 1 tsp. Ginger
  • 1 tsp. Nutmeg
  • 1 or 2 Eggs
  • 2 tsp. Soda Dissolved in a Little Hot
  • Water
  • 2 tsp. Vinegar
  • 3-4 cups Flour

Instructions & Notes

Cream shortening and sugars. Beat in molasses and eggs. Dissolve soda in the hot water and add with the vinegar. Mix in salt, ginger, nutmeg and enough flour to make a soft dough.

Preheat oven to 375°F. Drop dough by spoonfuls onto a greased cookie sheet. Bake 375 12 -15 mins.
