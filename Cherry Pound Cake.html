Cherry Pound Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Butter
  • 1 (8 oz.) pkg. Cream Cheese
  • 1 1/2 cups Sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp. Vanilla
  • 4 Eggs
  • 2 cups Flour
  • 1 cup Maraschino Cherries (drained) + 1/4 cup Flour
  • 3 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1/2 cup chopped Pecans

Instructions & Notes

Grease and flour an 8-inch tube pan. Blend softened butter, sugar, cream cheese, and vanilla until fluffy.

Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Gradually add baking powder and flour.

Toss drained cherries with 1/4 cup flour (to prevent sinking), then fold cherries and chopped pecans into batter.

Bake at 325°F for 1 hour and 20 minutes until skewer inserted in center comes out clean.
