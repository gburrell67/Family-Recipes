Carrot Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • --- Cake ---
  • 2 cups Sugar
  • 4 Eggs
  • 2 cups Flour
  • 2 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1 tsp. Soda
  • 1 tsp. Salt
  • 2 tsp. Cinnamon
  • 3 cups grated Raw Carrots
  • 1 cup finely chopped Walnuts
  • 1 cup Cooking Oil
  • --- Cream Cheese Icing ---
  • 1 8 oz. package Cream Cheese
  • 1 Tbsp. Cooking Oil
  • 1 tsp. Vanilla
  • 3 1/2 cups Icing Sugar
  • 1/4 tsp. Salt

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven to 325°F. Grease and flour a 9 x 13 inch pan.

Beat sugar, cooking oil and eggs together. Sift flour, baking powder, soda, salt and cinnamon and blend into the egg mixture. Fold in grated carrots and walnuts. Pour into pan.

Bake 325 for about 1 hour. Cool completely.

Icing: Beat cream cheese, cooking oil and vanilla until smooth. Gradually beat in icing sugar and salt. Spread over cooled cake.
