Rhubarb Cocktail

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 4 cups Rhubarb (chopped)
  • 6 cups Water
  • 1 cup Sugar
  • 1/4 cup Orange Juice
  • 1/4 cup Lemon Juice

Instructions & Notes

Combine chopped rhubarb and water in a pot; bring to a boil and cook until rhubarb is soft and tender.

Strain through a fine sieve or cheesecloth.

Stir in sugar, orange juice, and lemon juice while warm until sugar is dissolved.

Chill thoroughly in refrigerator and serve over ice.
