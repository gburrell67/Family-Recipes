Blue Magic Tart

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Fresh Blueberries
  • 1 can Sweetened Condensed Milk
  • 1/4 cup Lemon Juice
  • 1 Baked Pie Shell or Tart Shells
  • Whipped Cream (for topping)

Instructions & Notes

In a bowl, blend sweetened condensed milk and lemon juice together until thickened.

Gently fold in fresh blueberries.

Pour filling into a baked pie shell or individual tart shells.

Cover generously with whipped cream and garnish with additional fresh blueberries. Chill before serving.
