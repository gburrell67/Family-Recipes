Banana Cream Pie

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • --- Filling ---
  • 3/4 cup Sugar
  • 3 Tbsp. Corn Starch
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 2 cups Milk
  • 3 Egg Yolks
  • 1 tsp. Vanilla
  • --- Meringue ---
  • 3 Egg Whites
  • 3 Tbsp. Water
  • 1/4 cup Sugar
  • Dash of Salt

Instructions & Notes

Mix sugar, corn starch and salt together in a saucepan. Add milk. Cook over medium heat until it begins to thicken. Add egg yolks, beating well. Cook until thick. Add vanilla. Cool.

Place 1/3 of filling in baked pie shell. Add layer of bananas. Repeat, finishing with filling. Cover with meringue. Bake 425 until meringue is golden brown.
