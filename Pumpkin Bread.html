Pumpkin Bread

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3 Cups Sugar
  • 1 Cup Vegetable Oil
  • 1 Can (1 2/3 cup) Pumpkin
  • 4 eggs
  • 3 1/2 Cups Flour
  • 2 tsp. Baking Soda
  • 2 tsp Salt
  • 1 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1tsp. each of Cinnamon
  • Nutmeg and Allspice
  • 1/2 tsp. Cloves
  • 2/3 cup Water

Instructions & Notes

.Bake 350 for 45 mins. Makes 2 Loaves
