Midsummer Strawberry Dream

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 pkg. Vanilla Wafers (crushed)
  • 6 Tbsp. Butter (melted)
  • 1/2 cup Butter (softened)
  • 1 cup Icing Sugar
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1/2 cup chopped Nuts
  • 1 pkg. frozen Strawberries (thawed and drained)
  • 1 cup Whipped Cream (sweetened)

Instructions & Notes

Crush vanilla wafers and combine with 6 Tbsp melted butter. Press 1/2 of crumb mixture firmly into a 9x11-inch pan.

Cream softened butter and icing sugar together. Add eggs one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition. Spread evenly over crust.

Sprinkle with chopped nuts. Drain strawberries well and spread over top. Cover generously with sweetened whipped cream. Sprinkle remaining wafer crumbs on top.

Chill in refrigerator for 24 hours before serving.
