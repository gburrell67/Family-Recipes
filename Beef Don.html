Beef Don

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb Beef
  • 1/2 Onion
  • 3 Tbsp. Sugar
  • 3 Tbsp. Soy Sauce
  • 2 Tbsp. Sake or white wine
  • 1/2 cup Water
  • 1 Tbsp. Dashi-no-moto (Seaweed powder)

Instructions & Notes

Mix sugar, sake (or wine), water, soy sauce and Dashi-no-moto together. Bring to boil.

Add sliced (1 cm wide strips) onion. When onion is fully cooked add thinly sliced beef. When beef is cooked serve over rice bowl.
