Beef Don

Main Course

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb Beef
  • 1/2 onion
  • 3 Tbsp sugar
  • 3 Tbsp Soy Sauce
  • 2 Tbsp Sake or white wine
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 Tbsp Dashi-no-moto (Seaweed powder)
  • sake
  • wine
  • water
  • soy sauce and Dashi-no-moto together. Bring to boil

Instructions & Notes

Mix sugar
add sliced (1 cm wide strips) onion. When onion is fully cooked add thinly sliced beef. When beef is cooked serve over rice bowl.
