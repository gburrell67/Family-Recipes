Sunday Spice Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cup Flour
  • 1 1/4 cup Sugar
  • 1 tsp. Salt
  • 1 tsp. Cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp. Each of Cloves
  • Nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp. Allspice
  • 1/3 cup Mollases
  • 2/3 cup Milk
  • Beat Vigorously
  • Stir in
  • 2 1/2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Baking Soda
  • 3 Eggs
  • 1/2 cup Milk

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease two layer pans or a 9 x 13 pan.

Stir together flour, sugar, salt, cinnamon, cloves, nutmeg, allspice, baking powder and baking soda. Beat eggs with molasses and milk and stir into the dry ingredients until smooth.

Cook as two layers or in a 9 x 13 pan bake 375 for 35 mins.

Note: Mixing steps were added from classic similar recipes; the original recipe listed only the baking details.
