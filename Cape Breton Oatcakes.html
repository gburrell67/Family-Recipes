Cape Breton Oatcakes

From Family Recipes • Thin & Ultra-Crispy • Yield: 40 Bite-Sized Rectangles

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups (113g) Old-Fashioned Rolled Oats
  • 1 1/4 cups (150g) All-Purpose Flour
  • 1/3 cup (75g) Packed Brown Sugar
  • 1/4 cup (50g) Granulated White Sugar
  • 8 Tbsp. (1 stick / 113g) Unsalted Butter (melted and hot)
  • 2 Tbsp. Boiling Water
  • 1 1/4 tsp. Fine Sea Salt (or Kosher salt)
  • 3/4 tsp. Baking Powder

Instructions & Notes

1. The Coarse Pulse: Add 1 1/4 cups flour, 1/3 cup brown sugar, 1/4 cup white sugar, salt, and baking powder to food processor. Run for 10 seconds to eliminate lumps. Add 1 1/4 cups rolled oats. Pulse exactly 4 times to coarsely crack flakes without turning into flour.

2. Wet Bind & Knead: Pour 8 tablespoons hot melted butter and 2 tablespoons boiling water directly over dry mixture. Pulse 6 times until dough resembles wet sand. Turn out onto clean surface and press firmly into a cohesive, warm disc.

3. Template & Roll Ultra-Thin: Draw a 10x12-inch rectangle on parchment paper. Flip pencil-side down. Place dough in center, cover with second parchment sheet, and roll firmly to fill template completely (guaranteeing exact 1/8-inch thickness).

4. Flash Freeze & Slice: Peel off top parchment. Trim edges cleanly to match template lines. Slide dough on paper onto a tray and freeze for 15 minutes until solid. Slice cleanly into an 8x5 grid to create 40 small rectangles.

5. Airflow Spacing & Hot Bake: Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C) with Convection (or 325°F / 160°C Standard). Distribute 40 pieces evenly across 4 small black single-layer aluminum pans, leaving a 1-inch empty channel down center of each pan to break up steam pockets. Bake for 32 to 36 minutes until uniformly deep-golden, rotating pans front-to-back halfway through.

6. Residual Heat Setup: Let sit on hot pans undisturbed for 10 minutes to finish crisping. Transfer to wire rack for 20 minutes until completely cold and snap-brittle.
