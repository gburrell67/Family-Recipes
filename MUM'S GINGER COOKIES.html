Mum's Ginger Cookies

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 CUP BROWN SUGAR
  • 1/2 CUP WHITE
  • 1 CUP MOLASSES
  • 1 CUP SHORTENING
  • 1 TSP. SALT
  • 1 TSP. GINGER
  • 1 TSP. NUTMEG
  • 1 OR 2 EGGS
  • 2 TSP. SODA DISSOLVED IN A LITTLE HOT
  • WATER
  • 2 TSP. VINEGAR
  • 3-4 CUPS FLOUR

Instructions & Notes

BAKE 375 12 -15 MINS.
