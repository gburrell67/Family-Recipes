Cream Cheese Brownies

Appetizer

Ingredients

  • 1 pkg. 4 oz.Cream Cheese
  • 1 Egg
  • 1/2 cup Sugar
  • 2 Tbsp. Flour
  • 1/2 cup Maraschino Cherries--drained and cut up
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 cup of sugar
  • 3/4 cup All Purpose Flour
  • 1/2 cup of chopped Walnuts
  • pinch of salt
  • 1/4 cup of Cocoa
  • 1/2 cup Butter
  • Cheese Layer: Beat Cheese and Egg well. Blend Sugar and Flour in
  • gradually beating until well blended. Stir in Cherries. Set aside. Brownie layer: Beat Eggs until frothy. Add Sugar
  • Flour
  • Nuts
  • Mum

Instructions & Notes

Salt. Melt while stirring Butter and Cocoa. Add the rest of ingredients. Stir well.Spread2/3 of this in a greased 8x8 pan. Carefullyspread cheese filling over.Cover completly with remaining brownie mixture. Bake in 350 oven for 30-35 mins
