Spicy Raisin Oatmeal Cookies

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Shortening
  • 1/2 cup Brown Sugar
  • 1/2 cup White Sugar
  • 1 Egg
  • 1/2 tsp Vanilla
  • 2 Tbsp. Milk
  • 1 cup Flour 1 tsp. Soda
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt 1/2 tsp. Cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp. Nutmeg
  • 1 1/2 cups Oatmeal
  • 1 cup Raisins

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven to 350°F. Cream shortening and sugars. Beat in egg, vanilla and milk. Combine flour, soda, salt, cinnamon and nutmeg and stir into the creamed mixture. Stir in oatmeal and raisins.

Drop by spoonfuls onto an ungreased cookie sheet. Bake 350 for 12-15 mins.
