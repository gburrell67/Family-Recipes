Rhubarb Custard Pie

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Rhubarb
  • 1 cup Sugar
  • 1 Tbsp. Corn Starch.
  • 3 Egg Yolks
  • Creamy. Add beaten Egg Yolks. Cool

Instructions & Notes

Mix sugar and corn starch together.
Add to cut up rhubarb. Cook until creamy
Pour into a baked pie shell
Meringue: 3 egg whites 3 Tbsp. water beat egg whites and water until they form stiff peaks. Add about 3 Tbsp. sugar. Cover pie. Bake 425 until golden brown.
