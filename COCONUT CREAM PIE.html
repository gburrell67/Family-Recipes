Coconut Cream Pie

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3/4 CUP SUGAR
  • 3 TBSP. CORN STARCH
  • 1/2 TSP. SALT
  • 3 EGG YOKES
  • 1 CUP SHREDDED COCONUT
  • 2 CUPS MILK (BLEND)
  • MERINGE:
  • 3 EGG WHITES
  • 3 TBSP. WATER
  • 1/4 CUP SUGAR
  • CORN STARCH
  • AND SALT IN SAUCE PAN

Instructions & Notes

MIX SUGAR
ADD MILK. COOK UNTIL IT BEGINS TO THICKEN. ADD EGG YOKES AND BEAT WELL. COOK UNTIL THICK. REMOVE FROM HEAT. ADD COCONUT. COOL. PLACE IN A BAKED PIE SHELL. COVER WITH MERINGNE
BAKE 425 UNTIL MERINGNE IS GOLDEN BROWN.
