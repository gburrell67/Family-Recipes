Barbecued Pork Chops

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 MEDIUM ONION CHOPPED
  • 1/2 CUP WATER
  • 1/4 CUP VINEGAR
  • 1 TBSP. LEMON JUICE
  • 2 TBSP. BROWN SUGAR
  • 2 TSP. DRY MUSTARD
  • 2 TSP. SALT
  • 1/2 TSP. PEPPER
  • 1/2 CUP CATSUP
  • 2 TSP. WORCESTERSHIRE SAUCE
  • 4 PORK CHOPS
  • WATER
  • VINEGAR
  • LEMON JUICE
  • BROWM SUGAR
  • MUSTARD

Instructions & Notes

MIX ONION
SALT AND PEPPER IN A SAUCEPAN. COOK AND STIR OVER LOW HEAT UNTIL HOT. ADD CATSUP AND WORCHESTERSHIRE SAUCE. BRING TO A BOIL. REMOVE FROM HEAT. PLACE PORK CHOPS IN A BAKING DISH POUR HALF THE SAUCE OVER THE PORK CHOPS. BAKE UNCOVERED 350 FOR 30 MINS. TURN BAKE 20- 30 MINS. LONGER.
