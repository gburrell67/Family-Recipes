Cheese Ball

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 250 G. CREAM CHEESE
  • 3 OR 4 GREEN ONIONS (DICED FINE )
  • 2 OR 3 DASHES OF WORCHESTER SAUCE
  • 2 OR 3 DASHES OF SOYA SAUCE
  • 2 OR 3 DASHES OF TOBASCO SAUCE
  • 1 PKG. OF SUMMER SAUSAGE OR SALAMI
  • THEN ROLL IN REMAINDER SALAMI WHICH HAS BEEN FINELY CHOPPED.

Instructions & Notes

MIX CREAM CHEESE
ONION ALL TOGETHER. TAKE 1/2 OF THE SLAMI AND DICE VERY SMALL. ADD TO CHEESE MIXTURE. ROLL CHEESE MIXTURE INTO 2 BALL
