Nana's Gingersnaps

From Nana's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 CUP SHORTENING
  • 1 CUP MOLASSES
  • 1 CUP BROWN SUGAR
  • 1/2 CUP WHITE SUGAR
  • 2 tsp. Ginger
  • 1 TSP. SALT
  • 1 TBSP. SODA
  • 1 EGG
  • 2 TSP. VINEGAR
  • 3 1/2 CUP OF FLOUR
  • HEAT MOLASSES TO BOILING POINT. (I PUT IT IN MICRO WAVE FOR 1 MIN) ADD SHORTENING

Instructions & Notes

THEN ADD REMAINING INGREDIENTS. BAKE 350 -375
