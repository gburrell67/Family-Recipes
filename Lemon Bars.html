Lemon Bars

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Flour
  • 1/2 cup Butter (softened)
  • 1/4 cup Powdered Sugar
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 cup White Sugar
  • 3 Tbsp. Lemon Juice
  • 1/8 tsp. Salt
  • 2 Tbsp. Flour
  • 1/2 tsp. Baking Powder

Instructions & Notes

Cream 1 cup flour, butter, and powdered sugar together. Spread evenly into the bottom of an 8x8-inch pan. Bake for 20 minutes in a 350°F oven.

In a bowl, whisk together eggs, white sugar, lemon juice, salt, 2 Tbsp flour, and baking powder. Pour filling evenly over the hot baked crust.

Return to oven and bake for 25 minutes at 350°F. Allow to cool completely, then dust generously with powdered sugar and slice into bars.
