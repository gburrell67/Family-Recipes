Monster Cookies

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Butter
  • 2 1/2 cups Brown Sugar
  • 2 cups Granulated Sugar
  • 2 cups Peanut Butter
  • 6 Eggs
  • 4 tsp. Baking Soda
  • 1tbsp. Vanilla
  • 1 1/2 tsp Corn Syrup or Molasses
  • 9 cups Rolled Oats
  • 4 cups Total of: Smarties
  • Choc. Chips
  • Walnuts
  • Walnuts
  • Sunflower Seed
  • Wheat Germ

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven to 350°F. Cream butter, peanut butter and both sugars together. Beat in eggs, vanilla and corn syrup or molasses. Stir in baking soda. Gradually stir in the oats, then fold in the Smarties, chocolate chips, walnuts, sunflower seeds and wheat germ.

Drop large scoops of dough onto ungreased cookie sheets and flatten slightly. Bake 325-350 15-18 mins. Let cool on the sheet a few minutes before removing.
