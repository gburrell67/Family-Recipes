Banana Chocolate Chip Muffins

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 cup Mashed Bananas
  • 1/3 cup melted Butter
  • 1 3/4 cup Flour
  • 1/4 cup Nuts
  • 1/2 cup Sugar
  • 4 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 1/2 cup Chocolate Chips

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven to 400°F. Grease a muffin tin.

Beat eggs. Stir in mashed bananas and melted butter. In another bowl combine flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. Add banana mixture and stir just until moistened. Fold in nuts and chocolate chips.

Fill muffin cups. Bake 400 10 -15 mins.
