Banana Doughnuts

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup Shortening
  • 1 cup Sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp. Vanilla
  • 3 Eggs
  • 3/4 cup mashed Bananas
  • 1/2 cup Buttermilk
  • 5 cups Flour
  • 4 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1 tsp. Soda
  • 2 tsp. Salt
  • 1 tsp. Nutmeg

Instructions & Notes

Cream shortening and sugar; beat in vanilla and eggs. Blend in mashed bananas and buttermilk.

Sift dry ingredients together and add to banana mixture to form dough.

Chill dough thoroughly before rolling. Turn out onto lightly floured surface, knead gently, roll, and cut into doughnut shapes.

Fry in deep hot oil (375°F) until golden brown on both sides. Drain on paper towels.
