Blueberry Tea Cake

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 CUPS FLOUR
  • 2 TSP. BAKING POWDER
  • 1/2 TSP. SALT
  • 1/4 CUP SHORTENING
  • 3/4 CUP SUGAR
  • 2 CUPS BLUEBERRIES
  • 1 EGG BEATEN
  • 1/2 CUP MILK
  • TOPPING 1/2 CUP SUGAR
  • 1/4 CUP FLOUR
  • 1/2 TSP. CINNAMON
  • 1/4 CUP BUTTER

Instructions & Notes

BAKE 375 FOR ABOUT 40-45 MINS
