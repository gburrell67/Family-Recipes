Buffet Chicken

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 4 Chicken Breasts
  • 1/4 cup Butter
  • 1/4 cup Dijon Mustard
  • 1 tsp. Salt
  • 1/8 tsp. Garlic Powder or crushed Garlic
  • 1/2 cup Honey
  • 2 tsp. Curry Powder
  • 2 tsp Lemon or Lime Juice
  • 2 Tbsp. Prepared Mustard

Instructions & Notes

Preheat oven 350 melt butter in sauce pan. Add remaining ingredients. Pour over chicken. Bake 45 mins. baste occasionally.
