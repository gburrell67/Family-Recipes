ICE BOX COOKIES

COOKIES

Ingredients

  • 2 CUPS BROWN SUGAR
  • 1 CUP BUTTER
  • 2 EGGS
  • 3 1/2 CUPS FLOUR
  • 1 TSP. SODA
  • 2 TSP. CREAM OF TARTER
  • F.G. SALT
  • F.D. FLAVORING
  • CREAM BUTTER AND SUGAR
  • ADD EGGS. ADD DRY INGREDIENTS
  • CITRON
  • OR CHERRIES MAY BE ADDED. THESE ALSO MAY BE TOPPED WITH SPRINKLES. A GRAND CHILDREN FAVOURITE.
  • MUM

Instructions & Notes

A LITTLE AT A TIME. WHEN WELL MIXED
FORM IN ROLLS AND ROLL IN WAX PAPER. FREEZE OR LET STAND IN FRIDGE UNTIL STIFF. SLICE AND BAKE. NUTS
