Ice Box Cookies

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Brown Sugar
  • 1 cup Butter
  • 2 Eggs
  • 3 1/2 cups Flour
  • 1 tsp. Baking Soda
  • 2 tsp. Cream of Tartar
  • Few grains Salt
  • Few drops Flavoring (Vanilla or Almond)
  • Optional: Nuts, Citron, or Cherries
  • Optional: Sprinkles for topping

Instructions & Notes

Cream butter and brown sugar; add eggs and beat well. Stir in flavoring.

Add dry ingredients a little at a time until dough is well mixed. Optional nuts, citron, or candied cherries may be folded in.

Form dough into rolls and wrap tightly in wax paper. Freeze or chill in refrigerator until stiff.

Slice rolls thinly with a sharp knife and place on ungreased baking sheets. May be topped with colorful sprinkles.

Bake at 350°F for 10-12 minutes. A grandchildren favourite!
