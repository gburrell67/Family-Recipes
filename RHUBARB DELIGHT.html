Rhubarb Delight

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 6 cups fresh Rhubarb (chopped)
  • 1 cup White Sugar
  • 1 pkg. Strawberry Jello
  • 1 pkg. Mini Marshmallows
  • Topping: 1/2 cup White Sugar
  • Topping: 1/4 cup Shortening
  • Topping: 1/2 cup Milk
  • Topping: 1 Egg
  • Topping: 3/4 cup Flour
  • Topping: 1 1/2 tsp. Baking Powder
  • Topping: 1/2 tsp. Salt

Instructions & Notes

Spread chopped rhubarb evenly in a 9x13-inch baking pan. Sprinkle 1 cup white sugar over the rhubarb, followed by the dry strawberry jello powder, then cover evenly with mini marshmallows.

Prepare topping: cream sugar and shortening, beat in egg and milk, then fold in flour, baking powder, and salt. Spoon batter evenly over marshmallow layer.

Bake at 300°F for 50 minutes until golden and bubbling.
