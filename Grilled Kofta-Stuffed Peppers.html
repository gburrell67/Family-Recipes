Grilled Kofta-Stuffed Peppers

Source: Rebecca Firkser

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp. vegetable oil, plus more for grill
  • 1/2 cup quick-cooking couscous
  • 1 Tbsp. baharat
  • 3 1/2 tsp. Diamond Crystal or 2 1/4 tsp. Morton kosher salt, divided
  • 1 large shallot, finely grated
  • 5 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 1 lb. ground beef, preferably 20% fat
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped cilantro
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped salted toasted pistachios, plus more coarsely chopped for serving
  • 1/2 tsp. freshly ground pepper, plus more
  • 4 large bell peppers, halved lengthwise, ribs and seeds removed
  • 1 cup plain whole-milk Greek yogurt or labneh
  • 2 tsp. finely grated lemon zest
  • 2 Tbsp. fresh lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Prepare Couscous Base: Prepare a grill for medium heat; oil grate. Combine couscous, baharat, and 1 1/2 tsp. Diamond Crystal or 1 tsp. Morton kosher salt in a large heatproof bowl. Pour in 1/2 cup plus 2 tsp. boiling water. Cover bowl with a kitchen towel; let sit 5 minutes.
  2. Mix Meat & Fillings: Using a fork, break up couscous. Add shallot, garlic, ground beef, cilantro, 1/2 cup finely chopped pistachios, 1 1/2 tsp. salt, and 1/2 tsp. black pepper. Gently mix and set aside.
  3. Pre-Char Peppers: Drizzle halved bell peppers with 1 Tbsp. oil, salt, and pepper. Grill cut side down until just starting to char (about 3 mins). Let cool skin side down.
  4. Stuff & Grill: Lightly fill each bell pepper half with 1/2 cup meat mixture. Grill skin side down covered for about 20 minutes until filling registers 165°F.
  5. Serve: Whisk yogurt, lemon zest, lemon juice, and remaining 1/2 tsp. salt. Top peppers with pistachios and serve with lemon yogurt.
