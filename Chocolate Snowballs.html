Chocolate Snowballs

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup Butter
  • 1/2 cup Sugar
  • 2 tsp. Vanilla
  • 1 Egg
  • 2 cups Flour
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 1 6 oz. pkg. Chocolate Chips
  • 1 cup chopped Nuts

Instructions & Notes

Blend butter, sugar and vanilla. Beat in egg. Stir in flour mixture, chocolate chips and nuts. Shape into 1 inch balls. Place on ungreased cookie sheet. Bake 350 for 15-20 mins.
