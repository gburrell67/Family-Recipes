Orange Squares

From Mum's Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3/4 CUP SUGAR
  • 2 CUPS FLOUR
  • 1 TSP BAKING POWDER
  • 1/2 TSP. SALT
  • 2 CUPS FINE COCONUT
  • 1 CUP BUTTER
  • FILLING:
  • 1 CUP WHITE SUGAR
  • 2 TBSP. CORN STARCH
  • JUICE AND RIND OF ONE ORANGE
  • 1 CUP OF HOT WATER
  • 1 TBSP. BUTTER
  • WATER
  • SUGAR
  • FLOUR
  • BAKING POWDER
  • SALT
  • COCONUT

Instructions & Notes

COOK ORANGE JUICE
AND CORN STARCH UNTIL THICK. ADD BUTTER. MIX SUGAR
AND BUTTER TOGETHER.PLACE HALF THE MIXTURE IN 9 X 11 PAN. COVER WITH FILLING. THEN THE REST OF THE MIXTURE BAKE 30 MINS 375
